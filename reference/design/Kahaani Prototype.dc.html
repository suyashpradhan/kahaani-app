You can read her story below. Her original voice plays any time.

Our house was in Lakshmipuram. A small house. In front there was a jaguli — a raised porch.

In the evenings, all of us would sit out on that porch.

Amma would sprinkle water and sweep the yard. That smell of wet earth… I still remember it.

Sharada-akka from next door would come over. We would all sit together and just talk.

Back then the power went off every day at seven. We were never worried about it.

We would light the chimney lamp, and my grandmother would tell us stories.

Now that whole road has changed. Big, big houses.

No jaguli. Nobody sits outside anymore.

Two experiences, one system. The host curates the memory — people, question, her language, their own recorded voice, the private link. The storyteller only tells it: three states, no configuration, nothing to choose.
P0 is the whole spine: Family → person → question → your voice → link → her answer → memory. Anything marked P1 in the UI is deliberately inert.
The link carries storyteller, host, question, language and prompt audio — which is why her page has no switcher. Change the language chips here to see the same link in another script.
