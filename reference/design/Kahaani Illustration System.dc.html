Kahaani · illustration & motion system · v1 hackathon
Objects a family would recognise, drawn quietly, moving almost imperceptibly.
Six MVP assets, six motion patterns, one motif. The geometry, palette, layer structure and animation rig are built here and ready to paste. Faces are the one thing not drawn in code — those ship as portrait slots with a commission spec, because hand-built vector faces are how a warm product starts looking machine-made.
The motif · one story travelling
A spoken line straightening into a written one. It is the only decorative mark in the product: a divider between hearing and reading on the memory page, the spine of the processing animation, and the wordmark's companion. Never a spool, never a logo lockup.

02 Construction rules
Geometry. Everything sits on a 4px grid built from circles, rounded rectangles, triangles and single-curve paths. If a shape needs more than two curves to read, simplify the idea instead of the path.
Stroke. One weight per artboard: 2px at 128px wide, 1.5px below 80px, round caps and joins, colour clay or ink — never black. Objects are outlined and flat-filled; no gradients except a single 8% warm wash behind hero scenes.
Budget. Object ≤ 12 paths, cluster ≤ 40, character ≤ 60, hero ≤ 120. No masks unless an image is being framed, no filters, no embedded raster.
Layer contract. Every animatable part is its own <g id="…"> with its transform-origin authored in, so CSS can move it without touching geometry. Ids are kebab-case and stable: torso, eyelid-l, steam-1, canopy, ring-2.
Colour handles. Accent shapes use currentColor so a cluster inherits the surrounding UI tone. Skin, cloth and spice tints are literal fills, grouped as #skin, #cloth for swap-ability.
Export. Inline SVG in the component, viewBox only — no width/height attributes, no baked transforms, no ids from the drawing tool. One file per asset, 2–6 KB each.

Asset 4 Kitchen & recipe cluster
Recipe variant swaps the chai glass for a ruled paper rotated −4°, three ink lines drawn in with kSettleLine.
Purpose — the Recipes prompt and every food memory; the most-requested category in Indian families and the one that must feel lived-in rather than branded.
Composition — masala dabba face-on (seven katoris, spice tints), kadai in profile with two ring handles, chai glass with steam. Nothing garnished, no restaurant plating.
Artboard — 128×80. Dabba alone works as a 32px category icon.
Palette — mustard, maroon, clay spices; muted-blue steel; dusty-rose glass. 17 paths.
Groups — #dabba (#katori-1…7) #kadai #chai (#steam-1 #steam-2 #steam-3) #ground.
Static — dabba, kadai, glass. Animated — three steam paths only: 3s rise-and-fade, 0.5s stagger, never synchronised.
Appears in — prompt screen (Recipes), recipe memory headers, "Words from her story" when the word is a dish.
Asset 5 Listening visual
Purpose — says "Kahaani is listening", never "a device is recording". No bouncing equaliser, no red.
Composition — filled clay disc, four-path microphone in cream, two rings expanding outward and fading. Waveform sits below as a separate row of 28–40 bars so it can be driven by real levels later.
Artboard — 160×160 centred viewBox (−80 −80 160 160), rendered 116–172px. Bars are DOM divs, not SVG, so amplitude can bind to state.
Palette — clay disc, cream mic, clay-light rings, clay-tinted bars. One hue.
Static — disc and mic. Animated — #ring-1/2 3s ease-out, 1.5s offset; bars 0.6–1s alternate with 0.09s stagger. Idle state: rings off, bars flat at 12%.
Appears in — elder recording screen (116px), family recording screen (132px), and the same rings at 8px around a playing avatar.
Asset 6 A memory appears
Purpose — the "Keeping this memory…" state. Her voice becoming something the family can hold. Replaces every spinner in the product.
Composition — seven waveform bars collapse toward the centre as a card rises behind them; three ruled lines draw left-to-right; the motif's small wave stays at the card's foot, so the finished memory still shows a voice inside it.
Artboard — 200×120, rendered 200–260 wide.
Palette — clay bars and outline, ink ruled lines at 50%, cream card. One hue.
Groups — #bars #card (#rule-1..3 #wave). Timing: 4.2s cycle — bars hold to 40%, collapse by 70%; card fades 45→75%; rules draw 55→85% at 0.12s stagger.
Production — plays once and holds on the card when the memory is ready; loops only while still working. On failure the card outline turns dashed and the bars stay — nothing was lost.
Appears in — family "Keeping the memory" screen, elder "Keeping your story…" screen.
04 Motion lab — six patterns, all of them shy
1 · Breathing
translateY 0 → −3px, 6s ease-in-out infinite. Applied to #torso and to floating memory objects at 8s. If you can see it happening, it is too much.
2 · Blink
scaleY 1 → .08 for ~90ms at 95% of a 6.2s / 7.9s cycle. Coprime durations per character so two people never blink together.
3 · Chai steam
Three S-curved paths, translateY −18px + opacity 0→.8→0, 3s, staggered 0.5s so two wisps overlap. Only in kitchen and family scenes; never more than three paths.
4 · Leaves
rotate ±2.2°, 8s, transform-origin at the trunk base. One plant per scene, and never in the same scene as steam.
graphic_eq
5 · Memory waves
scale .72→1.5, opacity .5→0, 2.8s ease-out, two rings 1.4s apart. Runs only while a voice is playing or recording — it is the product's heartbeat, so it must mean something.
6 · A memory appears
The only narrative animation in the system, and the only one allowed to run longer than 4s. Plays once per saved memory; loops solely while work is still happening.
Global rules: transforms and opacity only — never width, top or filters. One looping animation per screen region, maximum two on screen. Everything stops under prefers-reduced-motion (this page included) and the assets must read perfectly frozen, because that is how they will be seen in a screenshot, a PDF and on a throttled phone.
05 Character family — spec, not stereotype
One rig, eight people, no interchangeable grandmothers. Each character is defined by two differentiators an illustrator cannot ignore — a silhouette cue and a posture cue — so they stay distinct at 46px. Nobody is drawn frail, nobody is drawn comic. Adaptable by design
Skin is a five-step ramp on a single #skin group, so any character can be re-tinted with one fill change. Drapes ship as three variants (Kannada/Tamil pleating, Bengali, salwar-dupatta); hair as four; body as three widths on the same skeleton. Headscarf, topi, turban and cross are available accessory groups — added when a family's story asks for them, never as default decoration.
The rig every character shares
#ground static · #torso breathe 6s · #head static · #eye-l #eye-r blink on unequal cycles · #pallu | #dupatta | #sleeve sway 7–9s · #prop static. Mouths never animate — no lip-sync, ever: the voice is real, the drawing should not pretend to speak it.
What to hand an illustrator
This page, the palette hexes, the two differentiator columns for their character, the 480×480 artboard with the 400px safe circle, the layer-id list, the path budget, and one instruction: draw someone's actual grandmother, at rest, with her attention on the person listening. Acceptance: reads at 46px, ≤60 paths, ids intact, no baked transforms, sits happily on #F7F2E9.
06 Memory worlds — object inventory
Worlds are built from clusters of three, never full scenes. Two are drawn (03, 04); the rest are specified here and drawn as the product earns them. Every world carries a town/city split so the product never suggests Indian memory is only rural — and no world leads with a monument.
1 · Childhood home drawn
verandah · courtyard · wooden window · trunk · charpai · steel vessel · plants · balcony · staircase · terrace · doorway. Town: pitched roof, jaguli. City: railing, parapet plant, grille.
2 · Street & lane
bicycle · tree · lane · shop shutter · distant house · school path · birds · evening light as a single warm wash. Three objects maximum; no poster skyline.
3 · Kitchen & recipes drawn
masala dabba · belan & chakla · steel utensils · pressure cooker · chai glass · kadai · serving bowl · handwritten recipe · spice jars.
4 · School days
school bag · notebook · fountain pen · slate for the oldest generation · bench · lunch box · bell · bicycle.
5 · First salary & work
office bag · pay envelope · ledger · typewriter only when period-true · bus ticket · train pass · desk lamp · wristwatch.
6 · Travel
coach silhouette with three windows — generic, no livery or logo · station bench · suitcase · steel tiffin · platform clock · window view. The richest world; build it second.
7 · Festivals modular
diya · rangoli arc · marigold string · lantern · thali · string lights · gift. Assembled per story, never a default festive skin, never one faith's set as "Indian".
8 · City memories
double-decker or route bus · balcony · market crate · park bench · old shop board · bicycle · monsoon puddle · low apartment block. Bengaluru, Mysuru, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, Delhi, Lucknow, Pune, Hyderabad get local cues — a tram wire, a hill road — not landmarks.
